Components, one by one

For building your own canvas, or updating a component in the file. Each is a C# Script component for Rhino 8.

  • FoldUI.csThe control window: every setting in one place
  • FoldDesigner.csBuilds the folded roof along a path
  • StressLines.csSTRESS: tension and compression in the roof
  • ModelKit.csLaser model kit: flat pieces, scores, tabs, labels
  • ConcreteFormwork.csFormwork panels, props and falsework drawings
  • TimberPanels.csMitred timber panels nested on stock sheets
  • FoldLabReader.csFOLDLAB: brings a Fold Lab design into Rhino

Adding a component

  1. In Rhino type Units and set Model units to Feet (answer No to scaling).
  2. In Grasshopper place a C# Script component and double-click it.
  3. Delete the template text, paste the whole .cs file, and close the editor. The inputs and outputs build themselves.
  4. Right-click the component and give it a name (WINDOW, STRESS, FOLDLAB...).

From the Fold Lab to Rhino

In the Fold Lab press Copy design. In Grasshopper, paste it into a Panel wired to the Design input of FOLDLAB. Wire its Plates into STRESS, FORMWORK or TIMBER, and its Supports into STRESS.
